# S3 world notes (WORLD-S3)

Date: 2026-10-14. Scope: `src/render/{styles/**,world,fx,labels,light,atlas,keys,map}.js`, tests, `e2e/world-*`. Nothing in `hud.js`, `ui/`, `game.js`, `main.js`, `input/`, `testhook/`, `audio/`, `core/`, `save/` was touched.

## What is in the world now
- Theme `executive` + room kind `boardroom` (floor kind added to `FLOOR_KINDS`, so every theme has it): wine carpet with gold inlay, mahogany walls with gold trim, glass walls onto a night skyline (wall variant 1, about 40%), award walls (variant 2). Darkness is lighter than the basement and the hero light is 1.5x wider here so the whole fight is readable. Dressing (render-only, **non-blocking**, placed from `gen/arena.js` coordinates): the big "quarterly results" screen behind the desks, boardroom table wings against the north wall, leather chairs, trophies. Light pools: `screen`, `desklamp`, `chandelier`.
- Boss `regional-director` (tier boss): own painter, canvas 188x196, sprites built **lazily** (`atlas.addLazy`) for both phases: key `mon:regional-director:<anim>:<dir>:<f>` (phase 1) and `mon:regional-director-rage:...` (phase 2: jacket off, suspenders, loosened tie, red eyes, red rim). Anims = idle, walk, attack, windup (4 frames: gavel-stapler raised), death. Aura on the ground (gold, red and faster in phase 2) and a boss light that follows it. The boss is never culled by the darkness and keeps its own colours.
- Telegraphs (`fx.drawBossTelegraphs`, drawn in `fx.draw`, i.e. above the darkness mask; progress is `(t + alpha) / total`): slam = dashed vermillion outer circle plus a hatched disc that fills from the centre, white edge flashes in the last 20%; volley = five dashed lanes with arrow heads and a yellow wedge that fills outward; summon = blue dotted floor ring with a shell of uprights that rises plus up-chevrons. Colour-blind safe by shape, hatching and fill growth, not hue alone (`palette.telegraph`, Okabe-Ito).
- `m.summoned` staff: dashed grey ring on the floor plus a small temp badge on the chest.
- Floor-8 stairs (`floor.bossStairs`): hazard-tape tile with a padlock while locked, lit gold tile with chevrons, rising gold rings and a warm light once `state.bossUnlocked`.
- Victory flourish: `bossDefeated` starts about 9 s of confetti and loose pages falling from the top of the screen, plus a short golden flash, a ring and a paper burst at the boss's tile. Also reacts to `bossSlam` (shock ring, shake), `bossVolley`, `bossSummon`, `bossPhase` (two red rings, pillar, shake).
- Style tests now iterate the bosses and both phase types (`test/render-pure.test.js`); new `test/render-s3.test.js`.

## Notes for UI-S3 / others
1. `hud.js` lines 55 and 126 read `content.monsters[m.type]` as a fallback when `m.name` is missing. Bosses are in `content.bosses` only. World uses `m.name ?? content.monsters[type] ?? content.bosses[type]`; hud should do the same (it only matters if `m.name` is ever unset).
2. Boss health bar: the world draws the normal name plate (name plus pip) for the boss; the big bar is yours.
3. `fx.handle` needs every core event (it already gets `bus.on("*")`); the new ones it consumes are `bossSlam`, `bossVolley`, `bossSummon`, `bossPhase`, `bossDefeated`. `bossDefeated` sets `fx.victoryAt`; the confetti is drawn from `fx.draw`, so the world must keep rendering during `scene === "victory"` (it does today, the panel sits on top of it). It is skipped when `fx.lowFx` only in density (70 pieces instead of 230).
4. Atlas build time grew (about 3.9 s of the 5 s budget at scale 2.5 in the bench). The boss sprites cost nothing at load (lazy); the first draw of a new boss frame costs about one sprite bake.
