# WORLD-R6 progress (round 6, render part of R6-2)

- done: N2 root cause: the skill effect lifetime is wall-clock render time (effects.js `now - born >= life`, `now` = performance.now() at the event and at the frame), not ticks and not reset by any event. On a running clock (`?realtime=1`, canvas sampled every 60-200 ms) the slam is on screen at 77, 121, 168, 351, 497, 664 ms and a faint ring at 858 ms (docs/shots/r6w-n2-slam-running.jpg). In the manual test clock time does not stop between steps, so a capture whose screenshots are >900 ms apart finds it gone (docs/shots/r6w-n2-slam-manual.jpg: gone at +913 ms real time). No code change needed; regression test added (render-r6 "N2"). next: N1.
- done: N1: every human monster and NPC now draws a face in every facing (walking away it glares back over the shoulder, face turned to the facing side; only the headset hero keeps a back of the head). Consultant skin/shirt/suit/tie/rim and Middle Manager skin/shirt one step darker. Root cause of the washed look was different: actors standing beside a wall or tall prop (`ghost`) were excluded from the pool-light restore (world.js `restoreActors`), so cool pools bleached them white; they are now restored (85 % of the usual share), and an actor faded because it stands in front of the hero is not restored (the hero stays readable). next: N3.
- done: N3: loot beams are ground effects drawn in the screen-space pass above the hero; when a beam overlaps the hero rectangle, fx.drawLoot calls `fx.heroRedraw` (world.js `redrawHero`, sprite + pool tint + hurt wash) right after the beam pass, so the hero stands in front of the beam. No cost when no beam crosses him. next: N4.
- done: N4: new `fx.onHead` keep-out (head rect, 15 z half width, 26 z tall, 4 px padding), checked in every plateFree pass, so the relaxed pass for rare, unique and hovered drops may still cover a body but never a head. next: G2 #8.
