## S3-1 Boss: The Regional Director in the Executive Suite

- Floor 8's stairs unlock when its last monster is killed (`bossUnlocked`, event `stairsUnlocked`; persisted). Emptying `state.monsters` by hand does not count. The stairs lead to **depth 9**, `floor.theme === "executive"`, name "The Executive Suite" (`strings.arenaName`), hand-built in `gen/arena.js` (34x38: a boardroom, a vestibule with the way back up, desks as the dais, four cabinets as cover). The arena has `exit: null` (no way down), `floor.arena === true`, one spawn `{type:"regional-director", tier:"boss"}`.
- Boss: `content.bosses["regional-director"]` (kept out of `content.monsters` so population, cheats and the style/audio tables are untouched; the core looks types up in `allMonsters`). Level 9 stats, `config.boss.hpMult 3`, `damageMult 0.7`. About 3,300 hp, immune to stagger, knock-back and Mute (event `resisted`).
- Phase 1: melee plus **Paper Slam**: a ground circle (radius 2.6) centred on where the hero stands when the wind-up starts, 54 ticks later it lands for 1.8x boss damage. Not rolled to hit: stepping out is the answer. Pattern id `p1`.
- Phase 2 at `config.boss.phase2At` (50%), once: event `bossPhase`, pattern id `p2`: a roar that **summons staff** (2 interns/consultants, at most 4 alive, every 720 ticks, level 6, they give no xp or loot), a larger faster slam (3.4 radius, 200 tick cooldown) alternating with the **Quarterly Volley** (5 slides in a fan aimed at the hero's position when the wind-up began, 36 tick wind-up, 0.55x damage each, rolled to hit). No randomness in the pattern.
- Reset: leaving the arena or dying rebuilds the floor, so the boss returns at full health in phase 1 (QF-04-9). A killed boss stays dead (also after a reload: `bossDefeated` suppresses the spawn).
- Loot: 3 items, rare or better, the first rolled as a unique (a rare of its base when all uniques are taken), plus gold. `config.boss.*` is the source; `config.bossDrops` is kept for the old test.
- Finale: boss death sets `bossDefeated`, kills the staff, clears hostile projectiles and starts a countdown; after `config.victoryDelayTicks` (150) `scene === "victory"` with `state.victory = {done, stats}` and events `victory` and `autosave {reason:"victory"}`. The scene name is **`victory`** as in AC-R-045-4 (the brief said "won"; the AC is binding). The simulation rests in that scene. `{t:"keepPlaying"}` (or `keepPlaying(state)`) returns to the **Lobby** with the character unchanged. The elevator still goes to floor 8 (`townTarget` is capped at `maxDepth`); `deepestDepth` becomes 9.
- `stats` (`kills`, `deaths`, `bossAttempts`) is new state, counted for the victory screen.

### Balance (careful bot, noted in the result JSON)

The bot now (a) restocks its belt from the bag, (b) rests on the cleared floor 8 to 95% health, (c) dodges telegraphs (it reads `boss.windup`). `npm run bot` (20 seeds, Lobby to boss, retries after a death): 20 of 20 win and press Keep playing; 6 deaths in the arena in total, so 14 of 20 (70%) win on the first attempt and none needs more than two. Boss fights last 89 s on average including the failed attempts (45-70 s for a clean win); the hero is level 14 with about 270-320 hp. A bot that does not dodge wins noticeably fewer first attempts (4 of 8 against 6 of 8 in the tuning set).

## S3-3 Save and load (core side)

Package `src/save/` (`format.js`, `migrate.js`, `storage.js`, `index.js`). Core stays free of storage; it only emits `autosave`.

- `serialize(state)` -> canonical JSON text with `checksum`; `deserialize(text)` -> `{ok:true,state,save}` or `{ok:false,reason,message}` (`parse|format|newer|no-migration|checksum|schema|size`), never throws. `loadSave(save)` builds the game; `check(raw)` validates and migrates without building.
- Saved (v1): `format, version, savedAtTick, seed, class, resume{depth}, deepestDepth, bossDefeated, bossUnlocked, townVisit, player{level,xp,attrs,attrPoints,skillPoints,skills,skillBar,gold}, inventory, equipment, belt, stash, cursorItem, uniquesFound, nextItemId, rng, stats, explored (run-length coded per floor), options`. Not saved: monsters, ground items, projectiles, floaters, hero tile and hp, dialogs, vendor stock, portal.
- Load resumes in the Lobby, or at the **entry of the saved floor** (`config.resumeAt = "floorEntry"`, QF-09-2); a save made while dead or on the victory screen resumes in the Lobby at full health (QF-09-3).
- Migration: `MIGRATIONS[0]` is the synthetic legacy shape (QF-09-5), fixture `test/fixtures/save-v0.json`.
- Checksum: `hashString` over the canonical text without the checksum (damage detection only). Required from version 1.
- Storage: `createAdapter(scope)` (localStorage when it works, else memory with `fallback: true`), `createMemoryAdapter()` (`failWrites` simulates quota), `createSaveStore(adapter, {onNotice})` with `save(state,{force})` (coalesces writes closer than `autosaveMinTicks` ticks unless `force`), `autosave(state, event)`, `load()` (a damaged save is kept once under `saveBackupKey` and the slot is cleared; a newer one is left alone), `remove()` (keeps options), `exportText("current"|"corrupt")`, `exportName()`, `importText(text)` (validated first, stored in canonical form), `loadOptions()/saveOptions()` under their own key.
- Failure notices: the store sets `state.ui.notice = {kind:"save", key, text}` (key in the string table: `saveFailed`, `importFailed`, `importDone`, `saveTooNew`, `saveCorrupt`, `saveSaved`) and calls `onNotice(key)`.
