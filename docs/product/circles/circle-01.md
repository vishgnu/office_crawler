## Questions (ordered by impact)

### Q1 ESCALATE - real customer: how close may names and visuals go to the original?
- **Why it matters:** decides title, naming, art, audio and marketing for the whole project, and whether the result can ever be shown publicly. The brief says "tribute" and "stay close to the original" (G2: partly contradicted). The harness rule forbids copying names, art, text and music. This is a legal/IP decision and is not delegated to `customer-proxy`.
- **Options:**
  - A. Strict: mechanics and genre conventions only; every name, look, sound and text original; title does not allude to the original; no screenshots or comparisons to it in public text.
  - B. As A, but the project description may call it "inspired by a classic isometric action-RPG" (without naming it).
  - C. Closer allusions: homage names, lookalike layout or UI. Needs a legal review first. Not recommended.
- **Proposed default (safe): A.** Working title "Office Crawler" stays internal only. Public naming and release are not decided here (also not delegated).
- Affects: R-102, NFR-L01-L05, R-070 (HUD look).

### Q2 What should it look like: perspective, art style, tone?
- **Why it matters:** the biggest cost and architecture driver. All assets are made by agents, so the style must be producible in code or from generated shapes, with consistent quality.
- **Options:**
  - (a) Perspective: true isometric 2:1 diamond tiles with depth sorting (matches the genre; harder: sorting, picking, occlusion); or top-down 2D (simpler, less "close to the original"); or top-down with a skewed 3/4 look.
  - (b) Art: pixel-art sprites generated by scripts from palettes and templates; or flat vector/geometric shapes drawn in code (canvas paths, simple animated figures); or hybrid (geometric characters, tile textures from a generator).
  - (c) Tone: dark-comedy satire (default); cosy comic; grim-serious office horror.
- **Proposed default:** isometric 2:1 projection, vector/geometric shapes drawn in code on canvas (no external art files, fully original, easy to colour-blind-test), dark-comedy tone, stylised non-gory violence.
- Affects: R-112, R-103, R-100, R-101, NFR-A03, NFR-P01.

### Q3 What is the MVP scope: classes, depth, boss, level cap?
- **Why it matters:** sets the size of the plan and the number of slices; the genre classic has several classes and dozens of levels, which is far beyond a first playable.
- **Options:**
  - Classes: 1 (melee) / 2 (melee + caster) / 3.
  - Depth: 4 floors / 8 floors / 12 floors, then boss.
  - Boss: 1 end boss with 2 phases / none (MVP ends at depth) / boss every 4 floors.
  - Level cap: 20 / 30 / 50.
  - Gear depth: all slots incl. durability / reduced slots, no durability.
- **Proposed default:** 1 class, 8 floors in 2 zones, 1 end boss with 2 phases, level cap 30, full slot list as in R-053, no durability, no identify. Further classes later.
- Affects: R-010, R-013, R-014, R-043, R-045, R-053, R-054.

### Q4 How much saving, and where?
- **Why it matters:** save/load touches every data structure; mid-floor saving is much more work than saving in town.
- **Options:**
  - A. Autosave to browser local storage at town, floor change, exit; resume in town or at floor entry; one character slot; manual export/import file.
  - B. Same plus exact mid-floor state restore.
  - C. No saving at all (single sitting).
  - D. Several character slots.
- **Proposed default: A.** B and D later.
- Affects: R-090-R-093, NFR-D01-D04.

### Q5 Audio: how much, and how is it made?
- **Why it matters:** no third-party samples (NFR-L01), so sound must be generated by agents; this is real effort.
- **Options:**
  - A. Procedural sound effects (Web Audio synthesis), simple generative ambient loop per zone, volume sliders.
  - B. Sound effects only, no music.
  - C. Silent MVP, audio later.
  - D. Licensed or CC0 audio files with recorded licences.
- **Proposed default: B in MVP** (procedural effects; music as "could" via A later). D only with licences recorded.
- Affects: R-080-R-083, NFR-P02.

### Q6 Monetisation, hosting and publication?
- **Why it matters:** decides whether accounts, payment, analytics or legal text are needed; and who may see the result. Publishing publicly is not delegated.
- **Options:**
  - A. None: free, no ads, no accounts, private/static preview only; public release decided later by the user.
  - B. Free public web page, no monetisation.
  - C. Any paid, ad or tracked model.
- **Proposed default: A.**
- Affects: NFR-D01, NFR-O02, NFR-L03, release planning.

### Q7 Target session length and difficulty feel?
- **Why it matters:** drives floor size, monster density, experience curve, loot rate and the save design.
- **Options:**
  - A. Short: full run to the boss in 20-40 min, moderately forgiving, death returns to town with no gold loss.
  - B. Medium: 1-2 hours, moderate death penalty (lose some gold).
  - C. Long: many hours, replay at higher difficulty.
- **Proposed default: A.** Replay difficulty later.
- Affects: R-006, R-023, R-031, R-043, R-048.
